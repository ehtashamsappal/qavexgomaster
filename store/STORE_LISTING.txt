FULL DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
Qavex Go Master brings the 4,000-year-old game of Go (Weiqi / Baduk) to
your phone — beautifully simple, fully offline, and completely ad-free.

PLAY YOUR WAY — 7 GAME MODES
• 9x9 Blitz — lightning games on a 5-minute clock
• 13x13 Battle — the perfect middle ground
• 19x19 Classic — the full-size board
• Vs AI — custom match: board size, handicap, your color
• Pass & Play — two players, one device
• Tsumego Puzzles — 8 hand-crafted life-and-death problems
• Timed — every second counts on a 10-minute clock

FOUR AI OPPONENTS
Easy for beginners, Medium for club players, Hard with 2-ply tactical
reading, and Expert with pattern play plus Monte Carlo playouts. All
respect the ko rule and Chinese (area) scoring.

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
• Interactive 6-page How to Play tutorial
• Live score estimator and move hints
• Handicap stones for fair learning games
• Kids Mode with friendly guidance

MAKE IT YOURS
• 8 hand-crafted board themes, including the signature Kaya Wood
  (kaya #E8C87A, ink, vermillion) plus Midnight, Ocean, Slate, Moss,
  Royal, Mono and Sunset
• 56 achievements, lifetime stats, daily streaks
• Stone Coins — soft currency earned purely by playing; spend them on
  themes. No pay-to-win, ever.

FREE, OFFLINE, PRIVATE
No accounts, no ads, no trackers, no data collection. Your progress
lives only on your device. One optional $0.99 "Buy Me a Coffee" tip
supports the developer.

Whether you're placing your first stone or chasing dan strength —
your move.
